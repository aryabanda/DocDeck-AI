This document covers management and entrepreneurship. Management is presented as a goal-oriented process involving planning, organizing, staffing, leading and controlling. It describes managerial roles and skills, Taylor's Scientific Management, Fayol's administrative management, systems and contingency approaches, motivation theories, leadership styles and the Managerial Grid. Planning includes objectives, premises, alternatives, evaluation, choice, action and review. Decision-making involves identifying problems, evaluating alternatives, acting and using feedback. Organizing includes span of control, organizational structures, departmentalization, delegation and decentralization. Controlling compares actual performance with standards and uses corrective action and feedback. The entrepreneurship units explain entrepreneur characteristics and types, business ownership, small businesses in India, sources of capital and support institutions such as DIC, KVIC, SIDBI, NABARD, NSIC, IFCI and IDBI. The venture-creation unit covers sources and methods for generating ideas, steps to start a business, business plans, the nine blocks of the Business Model Canvas, and an illustrative CampusBite meal-service business plan with marketing, operations, financial, human-resource and risk plans.
